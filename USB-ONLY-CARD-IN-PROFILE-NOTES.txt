ClassMind AI 6.4 — USB-only scanner update

Changes in this build:
- Removed camera UI, camera permissions/stream handling, and camera QR scanning code.
- Kept USB barcode scanner input on the attendance form. Most USB scanners act as a keyboard and send Enter after the scanned serial number.
- Moved the student QR card into the student profile modal. Open a student file to view the premium card and use its print button to print that student's card.
- Removed the standalone card button from the students table and the bulk card-print action from attendance.
- Other application features and offline QR generation remain unchanged.

No additional JavaScript libraries were added.
